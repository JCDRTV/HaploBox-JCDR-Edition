FIXES AND VISUAL REFINEMENTS – INGAME VERIFICATION STILL REQUIRED

1. BUG-28: QUICK SETTINGS MENU
- The Quick Settings flyout is parented to UIParent, independent of the
  main Controlbar, so Mini Heal mode no longer hides the menu's popup.
- The original main-view gear now explicitly registers LeftButtonUp.
- Mini Heal adds an independent small gear to the left of its MAIN button
  above the five HP blocks; this gear toggles the same Quick Settings flyout.
- The flyout is positioned within the local WoW client's current viewport;
  near the right edge it opens to the left and clamps to available space.
- The popup is independently rescaled when the client window is too small,
  without resizing secure Heal frames or the existing party UI.
- Toggle display updates even when layout size changes are deferred in combat.
- Switching MAIN <-> MINI HEAL automatically closes any open flyout, to
  prevent it covering the five mouseover-healing blocks.
- Slash fallback unchanged: /hb controls, /hb view main, /hb view heal.

2. CONSISTENT JCDRBOX CONTROLBAR ICON STYLE
- The visible controlbar heading now says JCDRBOX (addon folder and internal
  addon/DB names remain HaploBox for compatibility).
- Six new 64x64 transparent TGA glyphs for Settings, Loot, Auto Repair,
  Quest, Mini Heal and Frame Lock; white clean icon style matching existing
  Focus and Follow line-art. Backgrounds are dark, not inventory item art.
- The existing approved original Focus/Follow icons, status logic,
  shape, positions and colors are not modified.
